Metric definitions

tradedKwhEnergy traded between households, kWh per day
surplusSoldShareShare of all surplus in trading hours that was sold P2P (rest is exported)
demandCoveredShareShare of buyer demand in trading hours that was met P2P (rest is bought from the utility)
gainPerHouseholdEgpTotal benefit over exporting/buying from the utility, EGP per household per day
vwapVolume-weighted average trade price, piastres per kWh
pricePosition(average price - export rate) / (retail - export rate): 0 = sellers get only the export rate, 1 = buyers pay full retail
priceStdDevVolume-weighted standard deviation of trade prices, piastres per kWh
gridImportReduction1 - utility imports with P2P / utility imports without P2P
gridExportReduction1 - utility exports with P2P / utility exports without P2P
sellerRevenueGiniInequality of P2P revenue per kWp among prosumers (0 = equal, 1 = one seller takes all)
prosumerPremiumMeanMean benefit per prosumer over always-export, piastres per day
consumerPremiumMeanMean benefit per consumer over buying from the utility, piastres per day
surplusToDemandEnergy ratio: all surplus / all buyer demand in trading hours (above 1 = more supply than demand)
